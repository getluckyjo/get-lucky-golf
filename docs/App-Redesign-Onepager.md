# Get Lucky Golf — App Redesign

**Full redesign of the player app for public beta. Get Lucky V2.**

Get Lucky Golf lets South African golfers stake R50–R1,000 on a hole-in-one and win up to
R1,000,000, underwritten by Indwe Risk Services. The app works end to end. The visual layer
was never designed — it grew out of the build. We want you to design it.

Not a reskin: the visual language, the structure of every screen, iconography, motion, and
how the brand behaves in a product rather than on a poster.

## Three things that change how you'd approach this

**1 · Today's screens are a reference for what the app does, not how it should look.**
The 27 surfaces are a checklist of jobs the product has to do. If two should be one screen,
or one should be two, propose it — there's a template for that. Don't inherit any of the
current composition.

**2 · The brand is open, wordmark included.** The script lockup was drawn for print and
signage, where it's never asked to survive at 24px, sit in a tab bar, or animate. You may
repoint the palette and evolve the mark so it works in a product. It should still read as
Get Lucky — this isn't a rebrand. There is no icon-only mark today, and that's the single
most valuable thing you could draw for us.

**3 · Stage 0 is directions, not 27 screens.** Two or three whole-app visual directions on
the same two screens, with a short rationale each. We pick one, then you go deep. Loose
directions cost a fraction of one resolved screen and they're how we avoid a redesign
going the wrong way at screen twenty.

## What you deliver

Flat PNG comps at 750px wide, a redline twin with numbered circles, and a one-page card per
screen. Two token spreadsheets, already filled in with today's values — blank means
unchanged. A logo set including that icon-only mark. An icon set replacing the thirteen
emoji currently doing interface work.

## What's fixed

The 18+ gate blocks real-money play · the Indwe sponsor stays visible · legal links stay
reachable · the six stake tiers keep their exact amounts (R50→R25,000 · R100→R60,000 ·
R150→R100,000 · R250→R200,000 · R500→R500,000 · R1,000→R1,000,000) · payment hands off to
PayFast · 44pt tap targets · 4.5:1 text contrast.

Those are legal and contractual. Where they sit and what they look like is entirely yours.
